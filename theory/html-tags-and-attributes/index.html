HTML Tags and Attributes

HTML is built from tags that say what content is, and attributes that add detail a tag name cannot carry on its own. Choosing the right tag is the part that keeps mattering long after the page looks finished.

Side by side

The three, compared

Every row asks the same question of all of them, so you can read straight across to see what actually differs.

A tag says what something is, an attribute says something extra about it, and a semantic tag says what a whole section is for. The first two change what you see; the third changes what the page means.
